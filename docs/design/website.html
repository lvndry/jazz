LAW 01
Colour is semantics.
six hues, each answering "what is this?" — cyan is live, green worked, amber is a decision. nothing is coloured for decoration. ever.
LAW 02
Motion is allowed where text is not.
the room moves while you travel. the instant a sentence enters your eye-line, everything else goes still. animation everywhere — never under what you're reading.
LAW 03
Drawn, not pictured.
no illustrations, no stock 3D, no gradient orbs. every visual on the site is computed live from block cells — the same ▖▚▘ the terminal renders. the decoration is the tech demo.
